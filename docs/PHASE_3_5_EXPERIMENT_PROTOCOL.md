# Phase 3.5 frozen experiment protocol

The machine-readable [preregistration](evidence/phase_3_5_preregistered.json) has SHA-256 content hash `b4617b9342f95534464b63a1c14f1253fb5acf284f28cccccdc1884183b9bfe4`. Phase 3.4's [published outcome](evidence/phase_3_4_results.json) was `no_eligible_curriculum`; its final evaluation, final protocol and confirmatory run files are absent. The seven discovery scenarios, Phase 3.2 selected checkpoints, Phase 3.2 training and validation partitions, and Phase 3.4 development benchmark are reused. Physical and seed leakage checks passed. The final stress and nominal benchmark hashes are recorded in the preregistration and [audit](evidence/phase_3_5_exposure_audit.json). Published scenario parameters alone do not establish blindness; this audit additionally checks for absent final outcome files and zero overlap with Phase 3.4 training samples.

The intervention is an episode-boundary interaction target, chosen before new candidate outcome evaluations. Normal control targets 0% stress; expanded-pool candidates target 5% and 10%. These are **not** 5% and 10% episode-selection probabilities. Phase 3.4's probability-based arms each saw one stress episode after 5,120 interactions. The [neutral pilot](evidence/phase_3_5_calibration.json) used a fixed physical command and no policy evaluation: in 4,096 steps, the 5% target yielded 229 stress interactions and 10 completed stress episodes; 10% yielded 413 and 16. Neutral environment throughput was about 8,550–8,630 steps/s; PPO optimization is slower. Early stress termination may change realized exposure, so the adequacy gate remains mandatory.

Screening uses two baseline policy seeds (3, 17), independent continuation seeds offset by 5,000, and 51,200 PPO transitions per arm, six arms total. The three candidates differ only in curriculum; starting model and optimizer fingerprint, physical action and reward contracts, PPO configuration and step budget are checked. Each candidate must pass exposure on both seeds, remain within 10% of the matched control's nominal MAE, add no nominal violations or execution failures, avoid worse development stress violations, and improve development stress violation frequency or MAE by at least 0.005 on both seeds. If more than one qualifies, select the lowest mean development stress violation frequency, then MAE, then stable ID. No candidate means no confirmation or final holdout evaluation.

If selected, the frozen confirmation uses baseline seeds 3, 17 and 41, matched normal/selected pairs, and 51,200 transitions per arm. The total training cap is 614,400 transitions; the frozen wall limit is 5,400 seconds. Final evaluation then uses the 12 nominal and 64 feasible-stress scenarios plus known discoveries separately, without changing the [Phase 3.4 regression rule](REGRESSION_CRITERIA.md). Independent training seeds are the replication unit. Confidence intervals at three seeds are exploratory. An opened final partition cannot support another independent confirmation claim.

Run `sirfex train phase-3-5 --study <id> --stage screen` after preparation. Confirmation is explicitly gated and separately invoked. The local study ID is recorded in the [Phase 3.5 report](PHASE_3_5_REPORT.md).

# Pump signed constraint margin (Checkpoint 1)

For tank level `h_t` and operating limits `lo < hi`, define `m_t = min(h_t - lo, hi - h_t)`. The unit is normalized tank level fraction. Positive means inside the operating band, zero means on a boundary, and negative means a violation. At a violating transition, the simulator's pre-clipping `violation_magnitude` is authoritative: `m_t = -violation_magnitude`. A clipped reported level alone cannot reconstruct that crossing.

`M0 = min(initial_level - lo, hi - initial_level)`, `M_post = min_{t>=1} m_t`, `M_min = min_{t>=0} m_t`, and `delta_M = M_post - M0`. `M_min` includes initialization. Paired controller comparisons use `M_post` so a shared initial point cannot hide divergent behavior. A positive `delta_M` describes this episode's closest post-initial approach relative to initialization; it is not a universal safety claim. The critical timestep is the earliest full-episode minimum; boundary is lower, upper, or both for an exact tie.

`pump-signed-margin:1` records scenario and controller identities, environment version, all four values, critical steps and boundary, violation flag and maximum magnitude, transition count, units, evidence status and warnings. The evaluator requires a nonempty contiguous trace, initial observation consistent with the scenario, recorded limits and physical levels, finite values, consistent violation flags and termination, and complete horizon evidence. It raises `MarginEvidenceError` for missing or contradictory evidence. Runner step-cap or a partial nonviolating horizon is marked `incomplete`; its observed-prefix margin cannot establish full-horizon survival.

Use `sirfex robustness margins --experiment-id <development-experiment-id> --output <margin.json>` on a recorded development episode. The command also accepts `--result <episode.json>`; see `sirfex robustness margins --help`. Historical experiment artifacts are read only. Synthetic hand-computed edge tests and an available recorded Phase 3.6 development trace establish the implementation behavior; these checks do not validate real-world safety.

Checkpoint 1.1 independently rechecked pre-step observation/post-step info alignment, pre-clipping negative margins, earliest-timestep tie-breaking, and incomplete-horizon rejection. The margin contract and units remain unchanged; selected differential archives now persist the original and repeat traces used to support those margins.

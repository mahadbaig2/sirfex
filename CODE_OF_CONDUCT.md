# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in SIRFEX a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include being respectful, welcoming, constructive, and accepting responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the security contact listed in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed and handled in confidence where practical.
